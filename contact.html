Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our organization

How can I volunteer with SmartCare Africa?

We welcome volunteers in various capacities. Fill out our contact form above or reach us at info@smartcareafrica.org to discuss volunteer opportunities that match your skills.

How are donations used?

Donations directly fund our healthcare initiatives, environmental projects, and community development programs in Tanzania. We maintain full transparency and provide reports to our donors.

Can organizations partner with SmartCare Africa?

Yes! We actively seek partnerships with NGOs, government agencies, businesses, and educational institutions that share our commitment to health and sustainability.

Where does SmartCare Africa operate?

We are headquartered in Biharamulo District, Kagera Region, Tanzania. Our constitution authorizes us to operate at the national level across Tanzania.
